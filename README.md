# AU Underclassmen Opportunities 🇦🇺

A curated, community-driven list of internships, vacation programs, cadetships, research opportunities, and competitions **open to 1st–3rd year students in Australia**.

Inspired by [Underclassmen Opportunities](https://github.com/Jose-Gael-Cruz-Lopez/underclassmen-opportunities) (US) — this is the first Australia-specific version.

Most AU internship lists (like [AusJobs/Australia-Tech-Internship](https://github.com/AusJobs/Australia-Tech-Internship)) are aimed at penultimate/final-year students. This repo is specifically for students who still have 1-2+ years left — no "must graduate by [year]" gatekeeping.

**No experience required. Contributions welcome — see below.**
