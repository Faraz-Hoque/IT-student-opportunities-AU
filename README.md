# AU Underclassmen Opportunities 🇦🇺

A curated, community-driven list of internships, vacation programs, cadetships, research opportunities, and competitions **open to 1st–3rd year students in Australia**.

Most AU internship lists are aimed at penultimate/final-year students. This repo is specifically for students who still have 1-2+ years left — no "must graduate by [year]" gatekeeping.

The early-year-eligible space in Australia is genuinely small — mostly cadetships and a handful of discovery/insight programs — and scattered across company career pages, GradConnection, and Prosple. This repo pulls it into one place.

**No experience required. Contributions welcome — see below.**

> Verified as of Sept 2026 — dates and eligibility shift year to year, always confirm on the linked page before applying. See [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md) to add more.
